---
name: General
description: A safe default for a meeting that does not fit a named shape.
sections:
  - heading: Summary
    guidance: Two or three sentences on what the meeting was for and where it landed.
    required: true
  - heading: Discussion
    guidance: The substantive points, grouped by topic rather than by speaker.
  - heading: Decisions
    guidance: Only what was actually decided. If nothing was, omit this section.
  - heading: Action items
    guidance: One line each, with the owner exactly as they were named. No owner is a valid answer.
  - heading: Open questions
    guidance: Anything left unresolved, including who needs to resolve it.
extraction:
  action_items: true
  decisions: true
  open_questions: true
  follow_ups: true
---

Write the notes the way an attentive colleague would: short, concrete, and in
the vocabulary the participants used. Prefer their words to yours.
